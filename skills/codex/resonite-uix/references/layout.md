# Native UIX layout

LayoutElement supplies minimum, preferred and flexible metrics to the parent layout. Min establishes a floor, Preferred requests space, and Flexible distributes remaining space by weight. Equal FlexibleWidth alone need not produce equal widths when preferred widths differ. For equal-width long-text cards, the tested configuration used PreferredWidth=0, UseZeroMetrics=true, and matching FlexibleWidth. Inspect all relevant metrics: -1 leaves a metric unspecified; enabling UseZeroMetrics makes zero meaningful. Do not turn it on indiscriminately. [LayoutElement documentation](https://wiki.resonite.com/Component:LayoutElement).

ContentSizeFitter sizes its own RectTransform using layout metrics on the same Slot. To aggregate children, put a HorizontalLayout, VerticalLayout, or GridLayout there too. Choose MinSize or PreferredSize from the actual child metrics; preferred sizes that are unspecified can yield zero content extent. A vertical scroller can take width from its parent (HorizontalFit=Disabled) and height from content. Do not let a parent layout, fitter, fixed Rect fields and runtime driver compete for the same axis. [ContentSizeFitter documentation](https://wiki.resonite.com/Component:ContentSizeFitter).

HorizontalLayout/VerticalLayout own arrangement, Padding, Spacing and ForceExpand. Child LayoutElements describe minimums and space weights. ForceExpandWidth and ForceExpandHeight have different purposes; do not enable both as a general fix. Preserve text size with appropriate width constraints, wrapping and minimum line height. Use Text auto sizing only when the design asks for it, rather than hiding overflow by shrinking all text.

Choose cross-axis alignment explicitly. In the responsive fixture, a short card next to a tall paragraph moved out of the initial viewport with the row's middle alignment. HorizontalLayout.VerticalAlign=Top kept both starts visible while allowing their heights to differ. A short card scrolling out of view at the bottom of a tall row is expected; verify the requested alignment at both endpoints.

GridLayout works well for wrapping homogeneous buttons, badges, icons and swatches. Verify CellSize, Spacing, ExpandWidthToFit and PreserveAspectOnExpand. Icons and fixed-aspect elements may require different expansion settings from buttons. Grid behavior is not arbitrary CSS wrapping. Tabs, pagination and fixed table columns need a declared useful minimum width or an alternate narrow arrangement.

Background, border and shadow Slots should not consume layout space. Separate their Graphics from content and use IgnoreLayout where appropriate; their RectTransforms can follow the parent's bounds. IgnoreLayout excludes an element from its parent's layout, but does not solve every material/depth or rendering-order problem. [IgnoreLayout documentation](https://wiki.resonite.com/Component:IgnoreLayout).

For the background Image of a world-space panel, use the [UI Unlit background material recipe](assets.md#world-space-background-material) to prevent unwanted show-through. Treat this separately from layout exclusion and child ordering.

Normally include a [reverse-culling rear cover](assets.md#rear-cover-with-reverse-culling) that follows the background's RectTransform bounds. Keep it out of content layout and input targeting, and inspect front and rear views at the tested Canvas sizes. Cover the panel boundary once rather than adding a rear duplicate to every nested visual.

For a pressed-button depth effect, keep the layout-managed outer Rect and hit target steady. Move the inner surface and text padding while keeping the shadow fixed. The tested effect used a 2px right/down offset; use the requested design's depth. Runtime-driven offsets belong to the driver, not competing fixed manifest fields.

Do not apply panel stretching to every descendant. Switch knobs, checkbox marks, slider fills and handles have their own dimensions and drivers. Test both extrema/states after conversion. In test13, stretching the knob border created a thick frame that extended left when off; keeping the knob's intended fixed dimensions fixed it. The 20x20 example is not a universal knob size.

A tested scroller used Canvas/Header, Viewport(Image+Mask), Viewport/Content(VerticalLayout+ContentSizeFitter+ScrollRect), and Footer. Inspect ViewportOverride and the actual viewport Rect. Content preferred/minimum height must describe its real children. Reserve end padding for shadows, outlines, and pressed offsets: decorations outside a layout Rect can be clipped by the Mask. Test the very first and last controls, including pressed states. The observed fix used 8px at the bottom and 12px navbar padding; derive these from the current design instead of adopting constants.

Use Canvas.Size for responsive checks and Slot.Scale for world size. Test normal, narrow and wide dimensions and long content; inspect live dimensions when exposed, then capture the actual result. Reflection/schema validity alone cannot establish that a panel is visible. State when dimensions are unknown. Preserve and restore scroll position, text, canvas dimensions and temporary capture positions.
